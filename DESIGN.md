# Design System: M701 Room

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Sunroom Report"**

The dashboard behaves like a room-weather report pinned inside a sunlit kitchen: the first thing it does is tell you how the room feels, then it lays the channels out like a tidy row of labelled jars. The ground is apricot rather than paper, the reading surfaces are milk-white, and the type is one rounded humanist sans with enough weight to carry a friendly sentence. It is warm without becoming soft, and youthful without becoming a toy.

The page refuses the incumbent cream-and-serif instrument sheet. No monospace, no broadsheet hairline system, no laboratory black. Technical facts are set in the same friendly family as the headline, and state is carried by a short sentence, a badge, and an illustrated window rather than by a wall of color. The density is generous on the top of the page and tighter where history and raw readings need to be scanned.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Apricot page ground with milk-white reading surfaces.
- One rounded typeface across display, labels, and data.
- A state-responsive window illustration as the signature moment.
- A repeated three-zone comfort ruler under every channel value.
- Soft, warm-tinted elevation; pills and 22px cards; no hard offset shadows.

## Colors

A sun-warmed domestic palette: apricot ground, cocoa ink, milk panels, and a small set of fruit-and-garden accents that always carry data meaning.

### Primary
- **Sunroom Apricot** (`#f7e0cd`): the page field. It owns whole regions, never a scattered accent.
- **Window Peach** (`#f7bb8e`): the mid-stop of the hero panel gradient, from `#ffd592` through `#f7bb8e` to `#f3aa7d`; the whole gradient shifts with the verdict, from sunlit apricot to a hazier coral when the room needs air. See `hero-panel` in the frontmatter.

### Secondary
- **Tomato Coral** (`#de532d`): the single warm accent for the wordmark dot, focus rings, and the temperature series. It is a signal, not a background wash.
- **Marigold** (`#ffc145` / `#f59e3c`): the sun, the latest-row tint, and the "warm" end of the data family. It belongs to light, not to warning.
- **Leaf** (`#3d7a55`): the fresh/comfort state and the CO₂ and PM2.5 series.
- **Ochre** (`#b8791e`): the TVOC series. A darker **State Ochre** (`#9a5a06`) carries the elevated badge and flag.
- **Plum** (`#8a5b84`): the HCHO series.
- **Sky** (`#3e7fa8`): the humidity series and rain in the window scene.

### Neutral
- **Milk** (`#fffbf6`): every reading surface — metric cards, charts, table, empty state. It is warmer than white and never used as the page ground.
- **Warm Milk** (`#fff3e8`): secondary surface tint for table headers and hover rows.
- **Cocoa Ink** (`#2c1d16`): headings, primary values, and primary button ground.
- **Cocoa Soft** (`#523c2f`): secondary labels and table text.
- **Cocoa Muted** (`#775846`): metadata, units, axis ticks, and footnotes.
- **Rule** (`#efd9c5`): one-pixel separators inside cards, tables, and the masthead. **Rule Strong** (`#e3c3a8`): the short divider beside the wordmark. **Edge** (`#eac6a6`): the masthead and footer edges.

### Named Rules
**The Warm Ground Rule.** The page field is apricot; milk-white is reserved for surfaces that carry reading. A screen-wide cream or white field is off-world.

**The State-and-Series Rule.** Leaf, ochre, and red appear only as comfort state or as the matching channel series; they are never page decoration. Sun and coral are warmth and the temperature series. State is always named in words as well as color.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Gabarito (with Avenir Next, Segoe UI, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Gabarito (same stack)
**Label Font:** Gabarito (same stack)

**Character:** One rounded humanist sans for everything. Its open counters and slightly soft terminals keep the data legible while making the page feel spoken rather than printed. Weight, not a second family, carries the hierarchy. All numerals are tabular.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, 56px, 0.99, -0.038em): the verdict headline. It is a sentence with a full stop, max 18ch, and shrinks to 48px at 819px, 37px at 719px, and 33px at 430px. It is never a label.
- **Headline** (800, 27px, 1.05, -0.032em): section headings ("Right now", "History").
- **Title** (800, 20px, 1.2, -0.025em): table title and chart titles (15px for chart titles).
- **Body** (500, 16px, 1.55): the verdict detail, max 46ch. General page body is 400/15px/1.5.
- **Label** (600–700, 11–12px): channel labels, units, metadata, controls, and table headers. No uppercase treatment; no monospace.
- **Data** (800, 2.35rem, 1, -0.04em, tabular): the primary value in each metric card. Temperature and humidity keep one decimal; every other channel is an integer.

### Named Rules
**The One Voice Rule.** One family, many weights. A second display or mono face is off-system.

**The No Costume Rule.** Technical data does not earn a monospace face, uppercase micro-labels, or letter-spaced apparatus. Those are a costume for "technical," not a reading aid.

## Layout

The page is a single 1240px-max column with 64px gutters (32px below 719px). The masthead is sticky and 62px tall (56px on narrow screens), carrying the wordmark, connection pill, timestamp, and refresh action.

The first viewport is the hero: a two-column sun-card at 820px and above (verdict and detail left, illustrated window right), stacking below 820px with the scene centered under the copy. The readout begins directly under the hero as a four-column grid; the CO₂ card spans two columns, so the seven channels resolve into 2 rows of 4 slots at desktop. At 719px and below the grid becomes two columns, with CO₂ still spanning both. Charts are three-up, then two-up with the third full width at 1079px, then one-up at 719px.

Spacing rhythm: 4/6/10/14/16/24/40/52px. Tight inside a group, generous between groups, more space above a heading than below it. The page ends with a 58px-separated footer.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is softly lifted, not flat and not neubrutalist. Every reading surface sits on a warm-tinted shadow with a tiny contact line; the hero carries a deeper, wider shadow that lifts it off the apricot field. No hard offset blocks, no colored halos, no glass panels. The sticky masthead uses a functional translucent apricot wash with a 9px backdrop blur so content remains readable as it passes underneath.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`0 1px 0 rgba(70, 39, 22, .04), 0 18px 38px -30px rgba(70, 39, 22, .45)`): metric cards, charts, table, empty state.
- **Lift** (`0 2px 0 rgba(70, 39, 22, .04), 0 24px 48px -28px rgba(70, 39, 22, .42)`): metric-card hover only.
- **Hero** (`0 2px 0 rgba(70, 39, 22, .05), 0 30px 64px -46px rgba(76, 42, 22, .62)`): the sun-card.
- **Focus** (`0 0 0 3px #de532d`, offset 3px): every keyboard focus ring.

### Named Rules
**The Soft Contact Rule.** Shadows are warm, blurred, and descending. A zero-blur block shadow is off-world.

## Shapes

Corners are domestic and generous: 22px for reading cards and charts, 32px for the hero, 26px for the empty state, 24px for the table shell, and full pills (999px) for every control. Hairlines are 1px and warm; the only 3px strokes are the drawn window illustration. Borders never exceed 1px on a card, and there are no side stripes. The comfort ruler zones are pill-shaped; the marker is a 12px circle with a milk ring and cocoa keyline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 9px 14px 9px 12px padding, 12px/700 label.
- **Primary:** cocoa ink ground, warm-milk text, 14px stroke icon. Hover lifts 1px and deepens to `#3c291f`; active returns to y=0; busy spins the icon at 0.9s linear.
- **Range control:** a milk pill tray with 4px inset, holding three 8px 13px buttons with a 30px minimum height (32px on mobile). Hover tints warm milk; the selected button flips to ink/milk. The group is 100% wide below 719px with each button sharing the width.

### Chips
- **Status pill:** milk ground, 1px rule, 12px/700 label, 7px dot. Live is leaf; not-updating is ochre; waiting is ochre; offline is a pale red wash with red text. The pill is a live region and only announces when its label changes.
- **Verdict badge:** solid leaf, ochre, or red with warm-milk text, 12px/700, 7px dot. It is the first thing read in the hero and is never the only carrier of state.

### Cards / Containers
- **Metric card:** milk ground, 1px rule, 22px radius, 16px 17px 14px padding. Hover lifts 2px. Elevated and high states tint the card to `#fff8e8` or `#fff0ea` and tint the border.
- **Chart panel:** milk ground, 22px radius, 17px 18px 12px padding, 222px canvas (205px mobile).
- **Hero panel:** apricot gradient, 1px peach border, 32px radius, 46px padding (24–30px on narrow screens), with a masked dot grid in the right half. It holds the badge, headline, detail, three quick facts, and the illustrated window.

### Navigation
- No page navigation; this is a single-purpose screen. The masthead is the only chrome: 25px wordmark, 12px device meta, status, timestamp, refresh.

### Signature Component: The Comfort Ruler
Under every channel value sits one 6px, pill-shaped track divided into three comfort zones (leaf, ochre, red at 34–44% opacity) with a 12px series-colored marker. The marker's position is the value's place in that channel's full range; the zones are the channel's own thresholds. The ruler is `aria-hidden`; the same reading is exposed to assistive tech as a visually hidden level sentence. The legend is shown on desktop ("comfort / elevated / high") and hidden on narrow screens.

### Signature Component: The Window Scene
A drawn 232×178 window in a cocoa frame, with a sun, a cloud, rain, and a potted plant on the sill. Level 0 shows the sun and slow rays; level 1 adds the cloud and a 20% warm haze; level 2 adds rain, a 45% haze, and dims the sun. The scene is `aria-hidden`; the badge and verdict sentence carry the meaning. Motion is state-driven and stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Data Table
Milk ground, 24px shell, 53px sticky two-line header on `#fff0e2`, 37px rows at 12px/500, tabular numerals. The latest row is tinted `#fff3d9` with bold time; row hover is a warm-milk tint. The table scrolls to exactly ten full rows on desktop and eight on mobile so no row is half-cut.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the page field apricot and reserve milk-white for reading surfaces.
- **Do** let the verdict sentence carry the state first, then the badge, then the scene.
- **Do** use the same three-zone comfort ruler for all seven channels.
- **Do** set all numerals in tabular Gabarito and all labels in sentence case.
- **Do** keep focus rings at 3px tomato coral with a 3px offset.
- **Do** keep motion state-driven: live pulse, scene weather, refresh spin.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a second typeface, monospace data, or uppercase letter-spaced labels.
- **Don't** use a cream page ground, a display serif, or an editorial hairline system.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows, gradient text, or side-stripe cards.
- **Don't** use leaf, ochre, or red for decoration; they are comfort states or their matching data series only.
- **Don't** make the scene the only signal of room state.
- **Don't** use emoji or glyph characters as icons; draw them in the same 3px stroke as the window.
